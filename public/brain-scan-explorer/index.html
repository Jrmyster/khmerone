MNI152 / NIfTI

Ready to look inside?

Load the reference MRI when you are ready. The viewer and scan stay on this website; no account is needed.

Select a point in a slice to move the crosshair. Use the sliders for precise movement. In 3D, drag to rotate or use the turn buttons.

Orientation: L = left, R = right, A = front, P = back, S = top, I = bottom. L/R refer to the brain, not your hands. This viewer uses neurological display convention.

Move through the volume

Positions count voxel centers along the viewer's reoriented axes. They are not millimeters or anatomical labels.

Display contrast

Check your understanding

What this image can—and cannot—show

MRI data forms a three-dimensional grid of values. A slice displays one cross-section; volume rendering combines values into a 3D view. This teaching reference is not a diagnosis, a live brain, or a map of someone's thoughts. It does not represent every person's anatomy.

Patient-file uploads and drag-and-drop loading are disabled. No personal health data is requested.
