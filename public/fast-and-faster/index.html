SOURCES & THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK

01 / Exponential Math & Acceleration

  • Ray Kurzweil — The Singularity Is Near (2005) & The Singularity Is Nearer (2024).Law of Accelerating Returns; conceptual inspiration for the log₂ progress curve. The app’s piecewise rates and 1,000× integral are scenario choices.
  • Gordon Moore — Cramming More Components onto Integrated Circuits (Electronics, 1965).Historical semiconductor component-density trend; the simulator’s 18–24 month rates are illustrative simplifications.
  • Robert H. Dennard et al. — Design of Ion-Implanted MOSFETs with Very Small Physical Dimensions (IEEE, 1974).Scaling principles informing the 1975–2004 phase and the later power-wall and multi-core storyline.
  • T. P. Wright — Factors Affecting the Cost of Airplanes (1936); Jonathan Koomey et al. — Implications of Historical Trends in the Electrical Efficiency of Computing (2011).Learning-curve and computing-efficiency context for the separate, illustrative cost model. Wright studied aircraft; Koomey measured energy efficiency, not a universal $/GFLOP series. Swanson’s Law concerns photovoltaics, not semiconductor prices.

02 / AI Revolution & Takeoff Horizon

  • Tim Urban — The AI Revolution: The Road to Superintelligence (Wait But Why, 2015).Die From Fright analogy and the ANI → AGI → ASI narrative. The app’s DFF numbers are illustrative.
  • Ashish Vaswani et al. — Attention Is All You Need (NeurIPS, 2017).Transformer architecture behind the 2017 milestone; no fixed AGI date follows from the paper.
  • John Jumper et al. — Highly Accurate Protein Structure Prediction with AlphaFold (Nature, 2021).Published account of the protein-structure breakthrough represented by the simulator’s 2020 milestone.
  • Stanford HAI — 2025 AI Index: Research and Development.Documents rapid declines in task-specific model inference prices. Token prices are distinct from hardware provision cost per FLOP/s and training expenditure.

03 / Existential Risk & Astro-Engineering

  • Nick Bostrom — Superintelligence: Paths, Dangers, Strategies (OUP, 2014); Eliezer Yudkowsky — Artificial Intelligence as a Positive and Negative Factor in Global Risk (2008, MIRI copy).Conceptual AI alignment, recursive improvement, and existential-risk discussions; neither supplies the app’s deficit percentages or trigger thresholds.
  • K. Eric Drexler — Engines of Creation (1986).Molecular nanotechnology and atomically precise manufacturing concepts.
  • Nikolai Kardashev — Transmission of Information by Extraterrestrial Civilizations (1964); Freeman Dyson — Search for Artificial Stellar Sources of Infrared Radiation (1960).Civilization energy scales and possible stellar energy capture. Matrioshka brains are a later speculative extrapolation.

METHODOLOGY / SCENARIO BOUNDARY: log₂(Index) accumulates ∫1/Td(year) dyear. The default 2000–2100 integral is calibrated to 1,000 × the 1900–2000 integral by this app; it is not a published measurement. The independent cost curve interpolates log₁₀ dollars between illustrative anchor prices for one GFLOP/s of provisioned throughput; it is not a constant-dollar historical price series or measured cloud bill. Its 1947–1965 interval halves in exactly 2 years; the 2012 $0.08 → 2026 $0.001 anchors imply approximately 2.2 years per halving, so they cannot also represent continuous 8–12 month halvings. Inference dollars per token are a separate metric. Post-2030 costs approach a positive floor rather than zero. DFF anchors, 2027–2100 takeoff dates, 20CE warnings, and alignment scores are illustrative thought-experiment settings, not forecasts, measured safety levels, or values assigned by the cited authors. CLASSIFIED BRIEFING // THE MULTIPOLAR TRAP AND HUMAN SURVIVAL

As technological capability accelerates along a hyper-exponential curve, humanity approaches a critical existential threshold. The fundamental hazard of the Intelligence Explosion is not merely technological—it is game-theoretic.

In a fragmented world driven by geopolitical rivalry, international competition creates a 'Multipolar Trap'. Nations and corporations are incentivized to bypass AI alignment and safety verification protocols in a frantic race for capability dominance. When intelligence doubles in weeks or hours, unaligned superintelligence becomes a global zero-sum catastrophe.

Exponential AI renders classical warfare and unilateral containment obsolete. The margin for error drops to zero. Surpassing the AGI and ASI takeoff boundaries safely requires unprecedented international cooperation: global compute governance, transparent treaty verification, and an immediate halt to autonomous weapons development.

World peace is no longer merely a moral ideal—it is a strict biophysical requirement for the survival of Homo sapiens. Without unified planetary stewardship, coordinated global oversight, and absolute peace among sovereign nations, the capability explosion will outpace human governance, leading to irreversible existential collapse.
